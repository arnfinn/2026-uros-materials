# How our team analyzes bigfoot reports

- Reports before 1950 are sparse and mostly retrospective accounts. When
  describing trends over time, start in 1950 unless the user asks otherwise.
- The weather variables come from a model-based API, not from local weather
  stations. Treat them as approximate.
- A report count is not an estimate of bigfoot activity: it also reflects
  population density, BFRO's popularity over time, and media coverage.
